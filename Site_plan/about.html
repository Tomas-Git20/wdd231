About this website

Our Site Plan

The goals, audience and design decisions behind Argentina Through Time.

Site purpose

Argentina Through Time introduces readers to the nation's history from the independence declaration of 1816 through political, economic, social and cultural developments to 2026.

Target audience

The intended audience includes students, educators, travelers and general readers looking for an accessible introduction to Argentine history.

Scenarios

  • When did Argentina declare independence?
  • How did federalism shape the country?
  • What led to the restoration of democracy in 1983?
  • Which events shaped Argentina in the 21st century?

Typography

Georgia is used for headings to provide a traditional editorial style. Arial is used for body text and controls to support readability.

Color palette

Light blue #EAF4FB

Responsive wireframe

On desktop, navigation appears horizontally and content is organized into multiple columns. On smaller screens, navigation opens with a menu button and content cards stack vertically.
