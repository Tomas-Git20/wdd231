NP Membership Benefits

  • Designed exclusively for registered 501(c)(3) or local non-profit groups.
  • Basic listing in the online Chamber Directory.
  • Access to community networking mixers.
  • Access to Chamber resource guides.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Standard Chamber Directory listing with logo and link.
  • Invitation to monthly networking breakfasts.
  • 15% discount on Chamber workshop tickets.
  • Social media welcome highlight.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Includes all Bronze level benefits.
  • Eligible for random Home Page Spotlight advertisements.
  • 30% discount on event sponsorships and booth spaces.
  • Access to professional development training series.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Includes all Bronze and Silver level benefits.
  • Priority placement on Home Page Member Spotlights.
  • VIP table access at the Annual Regional Business Summit.
  • Free admittance to all training sessions and workshops.
  • Featured article in the quarterly Chamber newsletter.
